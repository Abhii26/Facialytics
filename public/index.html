UNDER THE SURFACE

A little science.
A lot of possibility.

Your original face-api.js pipeline, in a new workspace.

  1. 01

    Choose your input

    Enable your camera or select a photo. Images are processed locally in this browser tab.

  2. 02

    Find the face, map the details

    A lightweight face detector finds faces. Live landmarks use a dedicated video tracker. Recognition and optional estimates update separately in the background; photos use the original recognition models.

  3. 03

    Make a connection

    Conservative matching compares face descriptors with the gallery. Distant, ambiguous or low-quality faces stay Unknown. Live names require two consistent samples.

Webcam frames and selected photos are not uploaded or saved. The bundled reference photos are public assets. Estimates can be inaccurate; this is a computer vision demo.
